Our Story

Built on Purpose & Passion

Taveta, Kenya

"We started not just to run a school, but to build a place where children are truly seen, valued, and invested in."

Divine Favour Junior Academy was founded with a clear and heartfelt mission: to provide quality, holistic education to the children of Taveta and the surrounding region. From humble beginnings, we have grown into a nurturing institution that the whole community is proud of.

We operate under the Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC), delivering Pre-school, Primary, and Junior Secondary education to learners from diverse backgrounds. Our classrooms are places of warmth, curiosity, and high expectation.

Every child who enrols at Divine Favour Junior Academy becomes part of our extended family — and we take that responsibility seriously, every single day.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

The principles that guide everything we do — in the classroom, in the staffroom, and in the community.

Integrity

We model and expect honesty, transparency, and ethical conduct from every member of our school community — staff, learners, and parents alike.

Creativity

We celebrate curiosity and encourage our learners to think boldly, solve problems creatively, and express themselves with confidence.

Discipline

Self-discipline, responsibility, and respect for others are foundational habits we nurture from the earliest grades through consistent example and care.

Love for Learning

We believe that a love for learning, once ignited, lasts a lifetime. Our goal is to make every lesson engaging, relevant, and joyful.

Our Team

Dedicated Educators

Our teachers are the heartbeat of Divine Favour Junior Academy. They are qualified, passionate professionals who go above and beyond their job descriptions — building genuine relationships with learners and their families, and bringing creativity and care to every lesson.

Qualified Teachers

All our staff hold recognised teaching qualifications and are trained in CBC delivery methods.

Continuous Training

We invest in regular professional development so our teachers remain at the cutting edge of education.

Child-Centred Care

Each teacher is dedicated to the wellbeing and growth of every individual child in their care.

Find Us in Taveta

We are proudly rooted in Taveta, a town at the foot of Mount Kilimanjaro in the Taita-Taveta County of Kenya. Our school is part of the local community fabric, and we take pride in serving the families of this region.
