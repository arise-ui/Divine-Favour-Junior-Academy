Our Academic Programme

A rich, broad, and balanced curriculum under the CBC framework — building knowledge, skills, values, and attitudes in every learner.

Language Arts

English & Kiswahili
Core Subject

Language & Literacy

From the first letters of the alphabet to confident essay writing, our Language Arts programme builds strong readers, writers, and communicators. We teach both English and Kiswahili with equal care and depth.

Oral communication, creative writing, reading comprehension, and grammar are all woven into an engaging, progressive curriculum across all levels.

Reading Writing Oral Skills Kiswahili

Mathematics

Numbers · Logic · Problem Solving
Core Subject

Mathematics

We build a strong love of numbers from an early age. Our maths programme takes learners from counting and shapes all the way through algebra, data, and probability in JSS — always with an emphasis on understanding over rote memorisation.

Practical maths activities, games, and real-world applications make mathematics accessible and exciting for every learner.

Numeracy Algebra Geometry Data Handling

Science & Tech

Inquiry · Discovery · Innovation
Core Subject

Science & Technology

Our science programme is hands-on and inquiry-driven. Learners observe, question, experiment, and discover — building a genuine scientific mindset from a young age. We cover biology, chemistry, physics, and applied technology concepts across the grade levels.

At JSS level, learners begin to explore integrated science with a STEM focus, preparing them for senior school specialisation.

Biology Physics Chemistry Technology

Creative Arts

Music · Art · Drama · Crafts
Enrichment

Creative Arts & Expression

We believe that creativity is as important as any core academic subject. Our Creative Arts programme includes visual art, music, drama, and crafts — giving every learner a space to express themselves and discover their unique voice.

Arts education builds confidence, emotional intelligence, and collaboration skills that benefit every area of a learner's development and future life.
