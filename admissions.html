The Process

How to Apply

Our admissions process is simple, welcoming, and designed with families in mind.

1

Enquire

Contact us via phone, WhatsApp, or email to express interest and get more details about the school.

2

Visit Us

Schedule a visit to our campus in Taveta. Meet our teachers and see our learning environment first-hand.

3

Entry Interview

Learners undergo a brief, friendly entry assessment to help us place them in the right class and provide the right support.

4

Enrol & Begin

Complete registration, pay the applicable fees, and welcome your child to Divine Favour Junior Academy!

What to Bring

Admission Requirements

Documents Required

  • Birth certificate (original & copy)
  • Previous school report (if transferring)
  • Immunisation / health records
  • Passport-size photos (x2)
  • Parent/guardian national ID

Important Notes

  • Registration fees are payable at enrolment
  • Term fees are structured per section — ask for our fee structure PDF
  • Learners may join at any point in the term subject to space
  • We are inclusive and welcome learners with special needs
